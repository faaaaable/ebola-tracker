Other sources

The health zone boundaries drawn on this site's maps come from the “DR Congo – Health Zones” dataset published by OCHA DRC on the HDX platform, based on the Democratic Republic of the Congo's common geographic reference (Référentiel géographique commun). Each shape is a health zone, the reference unit of the official bulletins.
