Ebola in Haut-UéléHaut-Uélé

Situation as of 6 Oct 2026

As of 6 Oct 2026, Haut-Uélé province has recorded 364 confirmed cases and 153 deaths since the start of the outbreak, a case fatality rate of 42.0%. Cases are spread across 7 of the province's 13 health zones, mainly Wamba (119), Isiro (102) and Pawa (92).

3rd most affected province out of 7, with 4.2% of the country's confirmed cases. First confirmed cases on 25 June 2026, initially counted in Ituri: the bulletin only gives the province a line of its own on 10 July. Latest case reported on 5 Oct 2026.
