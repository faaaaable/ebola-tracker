Ebola in Nord-UbangiNord-Ubangi

Situation as of 20 Oct 2026

As of 20 Oct 2026, Nord-Ubangi province has recorded 3 confirmed cases and 1 death since the start of the outbreak, a case fatality rate of 33.3%. All cases are in a single one of the province's 11 health zones: Wapinda (3).

7th most affected province out of 8, with less than 0.1% of the country's confirmed cases. First confirmed cases on 16 October 2026. Latest case reported on 19 Oct 2026.
