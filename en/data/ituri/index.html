Ebola in Ituri

SitRep N°103 of 25 Aug 2026

As of 25 Aug 2026, Ituri province has recorded 4,750 confirmed cases and 2,133 deaths since the start of the outbreak, a case fatality rate of 44.9%. Cases are spread across 28 of the province's 36 health zones.

The country's most affected province, with 83.1% of confirmed cases. The outbreak started here: officially notified on 8 May 2026, declared on 15 May. Latest case reported on 25 Aug 2026.
