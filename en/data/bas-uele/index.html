Ebola in Bas-UéléBas-Uélé

Situation as of 6 Oct 2026

As of 6 Oct 2026, Bas-Uélé province has recorded 10 confirmed cases and 4 deaths since the start of the outbreak, a case fatality rate of 40.0%. Cases are spread across 3 of the province's 11 health zones: Viadana (6), Ganga (3) and Buta (1).

5th most affected province out of 7, with 0.1% of the country's confirmed cases. First confirmed case on 12 August 2026. Latest case reported on 26 Sep 2026.

The response

vaccination against Ebola

The response means all the actions taken to stop the outbreak: finding patients, testing and treating them, following up the people they were in contact with, and vaccinating those most at risk.

Vaccination

who is vaccinated and in which provinces

1,412People vaccinatedas of 6 Oct 2026

The Ervebo vaccine is reserved for those most exposed to the virus: health-care staff first, then other front-line workers — hygienists, safe burial teams — and, in Bas-Uélé, high-risk contacts under a so-called compassionate vaccination scheme. The campaign began on 26 August 2026 in Buta, then in Kisangani the next day; Ituri published its first vaccinations on 23 September.
