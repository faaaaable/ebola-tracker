Ebola in Sud-UbangiSud-Ubangi

Situation as of 6 Oct 2026

As of 6 Oct 2026, Sud-Ubangi province has recorded 2 confirmed cases and 1 death since the start of the outbreak, a case fatality rate of 50.0%. All cases are in a single one of the province's 16 health zones: Bulu (2).

7th most affected province out of 7, with less than 0.1% of the country's confirmed cases. First confirmed case on 10 September 2026. Latest case reported on 22 Sep 2026.
