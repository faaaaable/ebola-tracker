Glossary

The words used in the bulletins and on this site, as they are used here. Sixteen entries, from confirmed case to situation report.

A
Alert
A report of a suspected case or death to be checked. It is “validated” when it meets the suspected-case definition.
B
Bundibugyo
One of the ebolavirus species, distinct from the Zaire species of previous outbreaks. No licensed vaccine targets it.
C
Case fatality
The share of confirmed cases who have died. It is calculated on cumulative totals since the start of the outbreak.
Community death
The death of a confirmed case outside a treatment centre, at home or on the way. It signals a patient the response did not reach in time.
Confirmed case
A person whose infection is established by a laboratory test. Only confirmed cases are counted on this site.
Contact
A person exposed to a patient: family, carer, neighbour. They are followed for 21 days, the maximum incubation period.
Contact follow-up
The share of contacts actually seen each day by the teams. WHO aims for 95%; in the DRC the rate hovers around 85%.
E
Epicentre
The zone where the outbreak starts and where it is concentrated. In 2026, Bunia and Ituri.
ETC
Ebola treatment centre: the facility where patients are isolated and cared for. “Patients in isolation” means the patients who are there.
H
Health zone
The basic unit of the Congolese health system, 519 for the whole country. Each zone is divided into health areas.
P
Probable case
A patient or deceased person who showed signs of the disease and had a link to a confirmed case, without a tested sample.
R
Recovered
A patient discharged from an ETC after two negative tests 48 hours apart.
Response
All the actions taken to stop the outbreak: finding patients, testing and treating them, following up the people who were in contact with them, vaccinating, burying the dead safely and informing the public.
S
Safe and dignified burial
A burial carried out by a trained team, because the body of a deceased person remains contagious.
SitRep
The daily situation report of the INSP, the main source of this site. Each bulletin has a number.
Suspected case
A person whose symptoms suggest Ebola, awaiting a test. Becomes confirmed, probable or ruled out.
