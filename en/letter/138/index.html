Bulletin No. 138Situation as of 29 Sep 2026

Summary of the INSP bulletin, with each new release

← Previous letter, bulletin no. 137Next letter, bulletin no. 139 →
Today's figures

53 new cases, 28 deaths

The bulletin of 29 Sep 2026, published 30 Sep 2026, reports 53 new confirmed cases and 28 deaths. 53 cases is in line with the seven-day average of 57.7 a day. No new health zone since 18 Sep 2026: the outbreak remains in 63 zones across 7 provinces.

Since the outbreak began, 8,224 confirmed cases and 3,982 deaths: 48.4% of patients have died. 2,121 people have recovered. 16 more than the day before. 851 patients are hospitalised in Ebola treatment centres. Over the past seven days, an average of 57.7 cases and 29.0 deaths a day, against 59.4 cases and 28.9 deaths the week before.

02 Where

Ituri accounts for 32 of the 53 new cases. Then Nord-Kivu with 17 and Haut-Uélé with 4. The other 4 provinces report none. 5 zones carry 33 of these 53 cases: Bunia 14, Mandima 6, Beni 5, Butembo 5 and Nia-Nia 3. 44 of the 63 affected health zones reported at least one new case in the past three weeks; 19 reported none.

03 Deaths

Of the 28 deaths reported today, 22 occurred in the community, that is outside a treatment centre, and 6 in a treatment centre. Deaths outside a centre: 13 in Ituri, 8 in Nord-Kivu and 1 in Haut-Uélé. Those in a treatment centre: 4 in Ituri, 1 in Nord-Kivu and 1 in Haut-Uélé. The 4 in Ituri have not yet been assigned to a specific health zone, like 489 deaths before them.

04 The response

1,971 reports of possible patients ("alerts") were received; 1,971 were checked and 347 retained as suspected cases, to be tested. Of 328 samples tested in the laboratory, 53 were positive, that is 16.2 %. 73.5 % of the people who had been close to a patient (the "contacts") were seen by follow-up teams, below the 85% threshold the INSP has set itself. Ebola treatment centres are 103.1 % full: 365 patients for 354 beds. Beyond capacity: Nord-Kivu, 365 patients for 354 beds. Safe burials, carried out by protected teams to avoid contagion: 124 burials carried out and 104 bodies tested. The 47 deaths reported do not come from the same provinces: not all of them publish these figures, and the three numbers are not comparable. Around new cases, 34 prevention rings were set up out of 56 expected, that is the disinfection of the home and the follow-up of relatives. Vaccination: 6,897 people have received the Ervebo vaccine since the campaign began on 26 Aug 2026, according to the latest national total published, on 28 Sep 2026. Latest known figures by province: 4,953 in Tshopo (figure of 27 Sep 2026), 1,022 in Bas-Uélé and 731 in Ituri. Since the previous bulletin, 3 more in Bas-Uélé and 129 more in Ituri. At control points on roads and at borders, 252,041 travellers passed through and 97.3 % had a temperature check.

05 The difficulties encountered

In Ituri, some treatment centres lack space and food for patients, 15 bodies were not sampled for lack of supplies, teams or because families refused, and only 13 affected health zones out of 28 have psychological support. In Nord-Kivu, only one home in seven has been decontaminated (3 of 21), none received a kit and prevention reports arrive only half the time (9 zones out of 16). In Haut-Uélé, psychosocial workers lack transport in Wamba and Boma Mangbetu, and data comes through poorly. In Tshopo, use of health services is falling and the temporary closure of one facility is still proposed. In Bas-Uélé, there is still no compliant treatment centre and no ambulance, and vaccination has neither a validated plan nor funds.

Glossary · the words of the bulletin
  • Alert: a report of a possible patient or death, which teams go and check.
  • Area of the response ("pillar" in the bulletin): each of the specialised teams of the response, surveillance, laboratory, care, vaccination, hygiene, communication, security.
  • Confirmed case: a person whose Ebola infection is proven by a laboratory test.
  • Contact: a person who was close to a patient and whom teams follow for 21 days.
  • Control point: a post on a road or at a border where travellers have a temperature check and wash their hands.
  • Health zone: the basic health district in the DRC, usually built around a general hospital.
  • Prevention ring: around each new case, disinfection of the home and places visited, hygiene kits and follow-up of relatives.
  • Safe burial: a burial carried out by a protected team, as the body of a patient remains contagious.
  • Suspected case: a person whose symptoms suggest Ebola, awaiting the test.
  • Treatment centre (ETC): the facility where patients are isolated and treated.
