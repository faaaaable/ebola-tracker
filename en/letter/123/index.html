Bulletin No. 123Situation as of 14 Sep 2026

Summary of the INSP bulletin, with each new release

Today's figures

87 new cases, 35 deaths

The bulletin of 14 Sep 2026, published 15 Sep 2026, reports 87 new confirmed cases and 35 deaths. 87 cases is in line with the seven-day average of 84.0 a day. No new health zone since 10 Sep 2026: the outbreak remains in 62 zones across 7 provinces.

Since the outbreak began, 7,345 confirmed cases and 3,545 deaths: 48.3% of patients have died. 1,753 people have recovered. 27 more than the day before. 938 patients are hospitalised in Ebola treatment centres. Over the past seven days, an average of 84.0 cases and 39.7 deaths a day, against 81.6 cases and 37.1 deaths the week before.

02 Where

Ituri accounts for 57 of the 87 new cases. Then Nord-Kivu with 21 and Haut-Uélé with 9. The other 4 provinces report none. 5 zones carry 52 of these 87 cases: Bunia 20, Katwa 10, Mandima 9, Rwampara 7 and Nia-Nia 6. 48 of the 62 affected health zones reported at least one new case in the past three weeks; 14 reported none.

03 Deaths

Of the 35 deaths reported today, 26 occurred in the community, that is outside a treatment centre, and 9 in a treatment centre. Deaths outside a centre: 17 in Ituri, 8 in Nord-Kivu and 1 in Haut-Uélé. Those in a treatment centre: 4 in Ituri, 3 in Nord-Kivu and 2 in Haut-Uélé. The 4 in Ituri have not yet been assigned to a specific health zone, like 442 deaths before them.

04 The response

2,022 reports of possible patients ("alerts") were received; 1,568 were checked and 413 retained as suspected cases, to be tested. Sud-Ubangi did not send its figures. Of 409 samples tested in the laboratory, 87 were positive, that is 21.3 %. This share reaches 28.1 % in Ituri. 89.3 % of the people who had been close to a patient (the "contacts") were seen by follow-up teams, above the 85% threshold the INSP has set itself. Fewer than three contacts in four were seen in the following provinces: Sud-Ubangi. Ebola treatment centres are 48.3 % full: 583 patients for 1,207 beds. Beyond capacity: Nord-Kivu, 348 patients, 152.6 % full. Safe burials, carried out by protected teams to avoid contagion: 118 burials carried out and 110 bodies tested. The 140 deaths reported do not come from the same provinces: not all of them publish these figures, and the three numbers are not comparable. Around new cases, 113 prevention rings were set up out of 189 expected, that is the disinfection of the home and the follow-up of relatives. Vaccination: 2,960 people have received the Ervebo vaccine since the campaign began on 26 Aug 2026: 2,460 in Tshopo and 500 in Bas-Uélé (figure of 5 Sep 2026). Since the previous bulletin, 153 more in Tshopo. At control points on roads and at borders, 260,546 travellers passed through and 98.1 % had a temperature check.

05 The difficulties encountered

In Ituri, six treatment centres remain full on the suspected-case side, only 21 prevention rings out of 57 are open and the disinfection teams in Nizi are on strike over unpaid wages. In Nord-Kivu, 45% of patients are treated outside a centre, no bed is left in Butembo or Katwa, and five zones have no active disinfection team. In Tshopo, 18 control posts out of 30 are active, the patient who fled the centre is still at home and the incinerator is still broken. In Haut-Uélé, no communication material is left. In Bas-Uélé, still no treatment centre and no ambulance, and health workers in the five priority zones are not trained in infection prevention.

Glossary · the words of the bulletin
  • Alert: a report of a possible patient or death, which teams go and check.
  • Area of the response ("pillar" in the bulletin): each of the specialised teams of the response, surveillance, laboratory, care, vaccination, hygiene, communication, security.
  • Confirmed case: a person whose Ebola infection is proven by a laboratory test.
  • Contact: a person who was close to a patient and whom teams follow for 21 days.
  • Control point: a post on a road or at a border where travellers have a temperature check and wash their hands.
  • Health zone: the basic health district in the DRC, usually built around a general hospital.
  • Prevention ring: around each new case, disinfection of the home and places visited, hygiene kits and follow-up of relatives.
  • Safe burial: a burial carried out by a protected team, as the body of a patient remains contagious.
  • Suspected case: a person whose symptoms suggest Ebola, awaiting the test.
  • Treatment centre (ETC): the facility where patients are isolated and treated.
