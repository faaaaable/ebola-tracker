Outbreak timeline

A timeline to follow the key dates of the outbreak: the official milestones of the response and the thresholds crossed by the toll, computed from the bulletins archived on this site.

  1. Feb 2026
  2. 22 Feb 2026

    The virus is already circulating, unseen

    Reconstructed after the fact from sequenced genomes, the common ancestor of the sampled viruses dates back to late February, between mid-January and late March. The outbreak therefore ran for nearly three months before it was declared.

  3. Apr 2026
  4. 24 Apr 2026

    First suspected cases

    Cases of unexplained haemorrhagic fever are detected in Ituri province. Samples are sent for analysis.

    Open the province page
  5. May 2026
  6. 14 May 2026

    First situation report

    The first INSP SitRep devoted to this outbreak is published: it records 8 confirmed cases and 4 deaths.

    8 confirmed cases · 4 deaths
  7. 15 May 2026

    Laboratory confirmation and outbreak declaration

    Testing confirms Ebola virus, Bundibugyo species. The authorities officially declare the 17th Ebola outbreak in the Democratic Republic of the Congo.

    8 confirmed cases · 4 deaths
  8. 15 May 2026

    First case in Uganda

    Uganda confirms two cases imported from the DRC, on 15 and 16 May, in Kampala; one of the two patients dies. The first, a 59-year-old man from the DRC, had first sought care in Hoima District. Source: WHO, weekly report no. 1.

    8 confirmed cases · 4 deaths
  9. 16 May 2026

    Public health emergency of international concern

    The WHO Director-General determines that the outbreak, in the DRC and Uganda, constitutes a public health emergency of international concern, the highest level of alert under the International Health Regulations. Source: WHO, weekly report no. 1.

    8 confirmed cases · 4 deaths
  10. 17 May 2026

    Public health emergency of national concern

    The DRC declares a public health emergency at national level and activates its response: treatment centres, contact tracing, border screening points.

    13 confirmed cases
  11. 18 May 2026

    Public health emergency of continental security

    The emergency is extended to continental level, opening the way to a regionally coordinated response.

    13 confirmed cases
  12. 19 May 2026

    The outbreak reaches Nord-Kivu

    The province enters the bulletin table with three confirmed cases. The two previous reports do not mention it; the 18 May bulletin is missing from the archive, so this date is a lower bound.

    33 confirmed cases · 4 deaths
    Open the province page
  13. 21 May 2026

    The outbreak reaches Sud-Kivu

    “A newly affected province (Sud-Kivu), through Miti-Murhesa health zone”, the day's bulletin announces. The province will not go beyond three cases.

    83 confirmed cases · 9 deaths
    Open the province page
  14. 21 May 2026

    10 health zones affected

    First bulletin to give per-zone detail: it lists 10 — Bambu, Bunia, Mongbwalu, Nizi, Nyankunde and Rwampara (Ituri), Butembo, Goma and Katwa (Nord-Kivu) and Miti-Murhesa (Sud-Kivu).

    83 confirmed cases · 9 deaths
  15. 29 May 2026

    20 health zones affected

    22 health zones have now reported at least one confirmed case. New that day: Aungba, Damas, Gethy, Komanda, Lita and Mangala (Ituri) and Beni (Nord-Kivu).

    263 confirmed cases · 42 deaths
  16. Jun 2026
  17. 13 Jun 2026

    30 health zones affected

    31 health zones have now reported at least one confirmed case. New that day: Nia-Nia (Ituri) and Mabalako (Nord-Kivu).

    782 confirmed cases · 181 deaths
  18. 20 Jun 2026

    1,000 confirmed cases

    The cumulative toll passes 1,000 confirmed cases since the start of the outbreak.

    1,003 confirmed cases · 254 deaths
  19. 24 Jun 2026

    An imported case in France

    A doctor returning from five weeks caring for patients in Ituri is confirmed on his return to France. He recovers, with no infection among his contacts. Source: WHO, weekly reports nos. 7 and 8.

    1,155 confirmed cases · 304 deaths
  20. 25 Jun 2026

    The outbreak reaches Haut-Uélé

    Two contacts from the Nia-Nia health zone are found symptomatic in Wamba, then test positive. Their cases stay counted in Ituri, their zone of origin: the bulletin only gives Haut-Uélé a line of its own on 10 July.

    1,203 confirmed cases · 321 deaths
    Open the province page
  21. 30 Jun 2026

    The outbreak reaches Tshopo

    The day's bulletin reports the first case linked to the province: a 24-year-old woman, six months pregnant, who died at PK 51 in the Nia-Nia health zone and whose body was taken by motorbike to Kisangani, where the sample tested positive. The case is then attached to Nia-Nia, in Ituri, where it stays counted until 10 July.

    1,406 confirmed cases · 438 deaths
    Open the province page
  22. Jul 2026
  23. 10 Jul 2026

    40 health zones affected

    41 health zones have now reported at least one confirmed case. New that day: Wamba (Haut-Uélé) and Lubunga, Makiso-Kisangani and Mangobo (Tshopo).

    1,873 confirmed cases · 672 deaths
  24. 13 Jul 2026

    2,000 confirmed cases

    The cumulative toll passes 2,000 confirmed cases since the start of the outbreak.

    2,011 confirmed cases · 754 deaths
  25. 21 Jul 2026

    1,000 deaths

    The cumulative toll passes 1,000 deaths since the start of the outbreak.

    2,536 confirmed cases · 1,033 deaths
  26. 24 Jul 2026

    3,000 confirmed cases

    The cumulative toll passes 3,000 confirmed cases since the start of the outbreak.

    3,075 confirmed cases · 1,354 deaths
  27. Aug 2026
  28. 1 Aug 2026

    50 health zones affected

    50 health zones have now reported at least one confirmed case. New that day: Wanie-Rukula (Tshopo).

    3,748 confirmed cases · 1,657 deaths
  29. 5 Aug 2026

    4,000 confirmed cases

    The cumulative toll passes 4,000 confirmed cases since the start of the outbreak.

    4,053 confirmed cases · 1,851 deaths
  30. 9 Aug 2026

    2,000 deaths

    The cumulative toll passes 2,000 deaths since the start of the outbreak.

    4,381 confirmed cases · 2,011 deaths
  31. 12 Aug 2026

    The outbreak reaches Bas-Uélé

    “A newly affected province (Bas Uélé: Buta health zone)”: one confirmed case, one death. The sixth province affected.

    4,665 confirmed cases · 2,184 deaths
    Open the province page
  32. 16 Aug 2026

    5,000 confirmed cases

    The cumulative toll passes 5,000 confirmed cases since the start of the outbreak.

    5,021 confirmed cases · 2,378 deaths
  33. 27 Aug 2026

    End of the outbreak in Uganda

    Forty-two days after the last patient was discharged, on 16 July, Uganda has no more cases: the outbreak there is considered over. The country will have counted 20 cases, including 2 deaths. Source: WHO, weekly reports nos. 10 and 16.

    5,863 confirmed cases · 2,824 deaths
  34. 29 Aug 2026

    6,000 confirmed cases

    The cumulative toll passes 6,000 confirmed cases since the start of the outbreak.

    6,041 confirmed cases · 2,911 deaths
  35. 31 Aug 2026

    3,000 deaths

    The cumulative toll passes 3,000 deaths since the start of the outbreak.

    6,186 confirmed cases · 3,007 deaths
  36. Sep 2026
  37. 10 Sep 2026

    7,000 confirmed cases

    The cumulative toll passes 7,000 confirmed cases since the start of the outbreak.

    7,022 confirmed cases · 3,398 deaths
  38. 10 Sep 2026

    The outbreak reaches Sud-Ubangi

    “A new province has been affected, Sud Ubangi, through the Bulu health zone”: one confirmed case, deceased, a 23-year-old man who left South Kivu on 10 July. The seventh province affected, the first outside the north-east.

    7,022 confirmed cases · 3,398 deaths
    Open the province page
  39. 26 Sep 2026

    8,000 confirmed cases

    The cumulative toll passes 8,000 confirmed cases since the start of the outbreak.

    8,067 confirmed cases · 3,901 deaths
  40. 30 Sep 2026

    4,000 deaths

    The cumulative toll passes 4,000 deaths since the start of the outbreak.

    8,300 confirmed cases · 4,018 deaths
  41. Oct 2026
  42. 6 Oct 2026

    First case in Kenya

    A Kenyan who had lived in the DRC for seven years dies in Nairobi, where he tests positive for Bundibugyo virus. He had travelled through Kampala before flying. 28 contacts are being followed. Kenya becomes the fourth country affected. Source: Kenyan Ministry of Health, as reported by the press; not yet in an archived WHO report.

    8,728 confirmed cases · 4,205 deaths
  43. 6 Oct 2026

    Latest available toll

    The most recent bulletin included on this site reports 8,728 confirmed cases and 4,205 deaths.

    8,728 confirmed cases · 4,205 deaths
